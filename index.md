# Moon Tracker

Moon Tracker shows the moon for the city you choose: its phase and how much of it is lit, whether it's above the horizon right now, when it rises and sets, and how long until the next full moon. You can also set a reminder before the full moon.

Everything is calculated on your phone, and you don't need an account.

[Privacy policy](privacy/)

## Help

### How do I change my city?

On the home screen, tap the settings icon at the top right. That clears your city and takes you back to the country list.

### Why does the app ask for my location?

It only asks if you tap "Use My Current Location". The app reads your position once to find the nearest city in its list, then saves that city. If you'd rather not share your location, search for your country and city instead.

### I didn't get my full-moon reminder

Check that notifications for Moon Tracker are turned on in your phone's settings. Each reminder covers the next full moon only, so after it arrives, open the app and set a new one with "Notify Me".

### How do I change my ad choices?

If you're in a region where ad consent is required, such as the EU or the UK, tap "Ad Privacy Choices" at the bottom of the home screen.
