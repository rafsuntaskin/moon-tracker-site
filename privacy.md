# Moon Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Moon Tracker app on iPhone or Android.

## In short

- Moon Tracker has no accounts and no servers of its own. Moon positions and times are calculated on your phone.
- The city you choose and your reminder setting are saved only on your phone.
- If you let the app use your location, it reads it once to find the nearest city. The app doesn't save your exact location or send it anywhere.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device and usage data to show ads, as described below.

## Information saved on your phone

The app saves:

- the country and city you choose, with that city's coordinates and time zone
- your full-moon reminder setting (how far ahead to remind you, and whether it's on)

This information stays on your phone and isn't sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also clear your city at any time with the settings icon on the home screen.

## Location

Location access is optional. The app asks for it only when you tap "Use My Current Location", and only while you're using the app. It reads your position once, finds the nearest city in the app's built-in list, and saves that city. Your exact position isn't saved, and the app doesn't send it to us or anyone else.

You can pick your city from the list instead, and you can turn off location access for Moon Tracker at any time in your phone's settings.

## Notifications

If you set a full-moon reminder, the app asks for permission to send notifications and schedules the reminder on your phone. No notification server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere to deliver it.

## Advertising

Moon Tracker shows ads served by Google AdMob, a Google service. The app asks Google for non-personalized ads only, which means ads aren't chosen based on your past activity in other apps or on websites.

Even for non-personalized ads, Google's ad software in the app collects some information automatically to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

- your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
- device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android, and identifiers that are specific to this app or developer
- ad and app interactions, such as ads shown, ad taps and app launches
- diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs and app launch time

On iPhone, the app doesn't ask for permission to track you, so Google can't get your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) through this app. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad Privacy Choices" at the bottom of the home screen.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## What we don't do

We don't have accounts, ask for your name or email, read your contacts or photos, run our own analytics, or sell personal information. Apart from the ad data Google collects as described above, no information about you leaves your phone.

## Children

Moon Tracker isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Keeping and deleting information

Information saved on your phone stays there until you clear your city or delete the app. We don't hold any information about you, so there's nothing for us to delete. For information Google collects through ads, see Google's privacy policy. On Android you can also delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
